# Club Report

Intro:(Room no: 104)

The Music Club of IISER Kolkata is a community of students brought together by the love for music. From casual jamming sessions to large scale events throughout the year, the club acts as a space where our students can learn, perform and grow as musicians.

For many of us, the music room is much more than just a room filled with instruments. It is where friendships are formed, bands are created and ideas turn into performances. Whether someone has been learning an instrument for years or has just picked one up for the first time, the club provides a welcoming space to explore and enjoy music together.

Over the years, the club has grown not only in the number of members but also in the scale and quality of its activities. As students who actively use and care for this space, we hope to continue building an environment where creativity can flourish.
